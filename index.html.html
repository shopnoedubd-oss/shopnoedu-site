FAQ

Questions students and parents ask

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. You can book a free counselling session and profile assessment to understand your options before making any decision.

Which countries can you help me with?

We assist with admission and student visa guidance for Australia, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, Malaysia, Japan, Europe, Dubai (UAE) and Indonesia.

Do I need IELTS or PTE to apply?

Most institutions ask for proof of English proficiency, but requirements differ by university, course and country. Some accept alternatives such as a Medium of Instruction certificate. Tell us your situation and we will check the exact requirement for your shortlist.

Can I get a scholarship?

Many students qualify for some form of tuition reduction or funding based on academic results, the institution and the destination. We review your profile and help you apply wherever you are eligible.

What documents should I start preparing?

Begin with your academic certificates and transcripts, passport, CV and English test results if you have them. We will give you a personalised checklist covering the SOP, recommendations and proof of funds.

Can my parents join the counselling?

Absolutely. We encourage parents and guardians to attend so that costs, timelines and expectations are clear for everyone.
